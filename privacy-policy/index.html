Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026.

What we collect

If you fill in the service request form on this site, we receive the name, phone number, email address and job details you enter. That information goes to the shop so we can call you back. Nothing else on this site asks you for personal information.

How we use it

Only to respond to your request and to service your truck or trailer. We do not sell it, rent it or share it with anyone outside the shop, and we don't add you to a marketing list you didn't ask for.

Website analytics

We may use standard website analytics to see how many people visit the site and which pages they read. This is aggregate traffic information — page views, approximate location, device type — and it isn't used to identify you.

Cookies

This site uses only the cookies necessary to make it work and, if analytics is installed, to count visits. You can block cookies in your browser and the site will still function.

How long we keep it

Service records are kept as long as we need them to service your equipment and to meet our recordkeeping obligations. Form submissions that never turn into a job get cleared out.

Your requests

If you want to know what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, call the shop or email us and we'll take care of it.
