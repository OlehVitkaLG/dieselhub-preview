Same owner. Same technicians. Same location. New name.

We've been fixing heavy-duty trucks and trailers at 252 Patricia Ln in East Dundee since 2016. Until recently we did it as Lemberg Truck Service.

We started by keeping our own trucks moving

DieselHub opened in 2016 as the maintenance shop for our own trucking operation. Keeping our own units loaded taught us the one thing that actually matters in this business: a truck sitting in a bay is a truck not earning.

That's still how the shop runs. We schedule around your dispatch, we quote before we start, and we don't stretch a two-day job into a week because the bay is booked.

Why the name changed

We rebranded from Lemberg Truck Service to DieselHub Service. Same owner, same technicians, same bays, same phone number — just a name that says what we actually do. If you've been here before, nothing about the shop has changed except the sign.

Who we work on trucks for

Owner-operators and fleets across the Chicago area. Some units come in once for an inspection; some have been on a PM schedule with us for years. We service Volvo, Freightliner, Peterbilt and Kenworth tractors, and dry van, reefer, flatbed and open deck trailers.

We're also a credentialed CARB Clean Truck Check tester, which means drivers running California freight can get tested here in Illinois instead of losing two days on the road.

How we'd like to be judged

  • The estimate matches the invoice. If it can't, you hear why before the work happens, not after.
  • Labor, hours and parts are broken out. Nothing hidden in a shop-supply line.
  • We say no when it's a no. We don't do roadside, towing or body work, and we'll tell you that on the phone rather than let you tow a unit here for nothing.
  • The same people see your truck next time. That's the whole advantage of a shop this size.

Where to find us

252 Patricia Ln, East Dundee, IL 60118 — minutes off I-90, on Route 25/72. Open Monday through Friday 8 AM to 5 PM, Saturday 8 AM to 2 PM.
