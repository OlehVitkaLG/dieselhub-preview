What a fleet account gets you

  • PM intervals set to your mileage, not our calendar. We schedule against how your units actually run, and we call you when a unit is coming due instead of waiting for you to notice.
  • Per-unit invoicing. Every invoice is tied to a truck number, with labor, hours and parts broken out. At the end of a quarter you can see which unit is eating your margin.
  • Service history in one place. What was done, when, at what mileage. Useful when you're deciding whether to keep a unit or trade it.
  • Trucks and trailers, same shop. Including structural welding and trailer repair, so you're not splitting a unit across two vendors.
  • Priority scheduling for account units. A truck on an account gets slotted ahead of a walk-in.
  • One estimate process, every time. Written before the work, and a call before anything changes. No surprise line items to argue about at month end.

What we handle for fleets

The whole in-house list is on the services page. For fleets, the jobs that come up on repeat are these:

  • Preventive maintenance on a set interval
  • DOT inspections — annual, scheduled in batches so you're not chasing them
  • CARB Clean Truck Check for units that run California, done in Illinois
  • Brakes and ABS, suspension, tires
  • DPF and aftertreatment before it becomes a derate on the road
  • Trailer repair and welding — dry van, reefer, flatbed, open deck

Straight about what we're not

We're a shop, not a nationwide network. There's no roadside division and no mobile unit — if a truck goes down on the road, it needs a tow, and we'll take it from there. We're not going to pretend otherwise to win an account and then disappoint you at 2 a.m.

What we are good at: being the shop your units come back to, with the same technicians who saw them last time.

Starting an account

  • Call or email with your unit count and makes. Trucks, trailers, and roughly how they run — regional, OTR, running California or not.
  • We set the PM schedule together. Based on your mileage and what your insurance or customers require.
  • Start with one unit if you want. Most accounts here began as one truck that came in for something else.
