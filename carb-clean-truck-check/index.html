Why get tested here instead of out west

Most drivers running California freight treat Clean Truck Check as something to deal with when they're already out there. That's how a compliance test turns into two lost days: you're hunting a tester in an unfamiliar market, on their schedule, and if the truck fails you're finding a repair shop you've never used.

Doing it in East Dundee removes all of that. You book around your own dispatch, you're in a shop that already knows your unit, and if something fails we're the ones who fix it. No second appointment, no second location.

What the test actually is

Clean Truck Check is California's periodic emissions compliance program for heavy-duty vehicles. For most trucks it means a periodic OBD test submitted electronically to CARB by a credentialed tester. Older units without OBD go through an opacity test instead.

  • We're credentialed to perform and submit the test. Not a middleman — the testing happens here.
  • Results are submitted electronically. You don't leave holding paperwork you have to chase.
  • Failures get fixed in the same shop. Aftertreatment, DPF, EGR, sensors, wiring — that's already what we do all day.

Common reasons a truck fails

If your dash has been showing any of these, expect trouble at the test and bring it in early rather than after:

  • Check engine light on
  • MIL commanded on in OBD data
  • Active or pending emissions DTCs
  • Incomplete readiness monitors
  • Recent ECM reflash or battery disconnect
  • Failed regens / DPF light
  • Derate warnings
  • Missing or tampered aftertreatment components

A cleared code is not a passed test. Clearing codes resets the readiness monitors, and a truck with incomplete monitors can't pass — which is why "I just cleared it this morning" is the single most common reason a unit gets sent away. Drive cycles take time. Come in with the monitors already run.
