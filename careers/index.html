What it's actually like here

  • Shop work only. No roadside, no mobile calls, no towing. Every job happens in the bay with your tools where you left them.
  • Predictable schedule. Mon–Fri 8–5, Sat 8–2, Sunday closed. Overtime is offered, not assumed.
  • Whole units, not one task. You'll see engines, aftertreatment, drivetrain, brakes, electrical and trailers — not the same job forty times a week.
  • The owner is in the shop. Greg's here most days. Decisions don't go through three layers.
  • Small crew. That means your work is visible and so is your effort.

Who we're looking for

Experience matters more than paper here, but you should be able to read a wiring diagram, pull and interpret codes, and finish a job without someone checking on you. If you're strong on aftertreatment and diagnostics, tell us — that's where the demand is.

  • Heavy-duty diesel experience — Volvo, Freightliner, Peterbilt, Kenworth
  • Comfortable with computer diagnostics, not just mechanical work
  • Your own hand tools (we supply the heavy equipment)
  • Valid driver's licence; CDL is a plus, not a requirement
  • Straight with customers and with us

How to apply

Call the shop and ask for Greg, or email your experience to service@dieselhubservice.com. A resume is welcome but not required — tell us what you've worked on and where. Walking in during shop hours also works.
