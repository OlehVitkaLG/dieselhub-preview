Bring it in if you're seeing this

These are the words drivers use when they call us about preventive maintenance. If any of them describe your unit, describe it to us the same way on the phone — it gets us to the answer faster.

  • PM interval due
  • Oil change overdue
  • Don't know when it was last serviced
  • Getting a fleet onto a schedule

What we do

  • Oil and filter service, fuel and air filters, coolant filter.
  • Full chassis greasing, driveline and fifth wheel.
  • Fluid levels and condition: transmission, differentials, power steering, coolant.
  • Brake, tire, suspension, steering, lighting and air system inspection.
  • Belt, hose, leak and battery check.
  • A written findings list ranked by what needs doing now and what can wait.

How long it takes

A PM service is a same-day job. If the inspection turns up work, you get the estimate before anything beyond the PM happens.

Worth knowing

Stack your compliance work onto the PM. A PM, a DOT inspection and a CARB test in one visit is one day of downtime instead of three.

How the job runs

  • You call or send it in. Describe the symptom — the noise, the code, the light, what changed.
  • We diagnose and quote. Written estimate with labor, hours and parts broken out, before we start.
  • You approve, we work. If we find something else, you hear it before it lands on the invoice.
  • You leave with the paperwork. Itemized invoice and any documentation the job required.
