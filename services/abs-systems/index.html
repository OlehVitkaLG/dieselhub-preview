Truck & Trailer ABS Repair — East Dundee, IL

An ABS lamp that stays on is a violation, and on a trailer it's one an inspector can see from outside the truck. Most ABS faults come down to a wheel speed sensor, a tone ring or a chafed wire — cheap to fix once someone actually traces it.

Bring it in if you're seeing this

These are the words drivers use when they call us about abs systems. If any of them describe your unit, describe it to us the same way on the phone — it gets us to the answer faster.

  • ABS lamp on, tractor or trailer
  • Trailer ABS lamp stays lit
  • ABS fault codes stored
  • Wheel lockup under braking
  • Lamp comes on intermittently
  • Came on after wheel-end work

What we do

  • ABS code pull and live wheel speed data on tractor and trailer.
  • Wheel speed sensors, sensor clamps, tone ring inspection and replacement.
  • Modulator valves and ABS ECU diagnosis.
  • Wiring, connector and ground repair — the cause of most intermittent faults.
  • Post-repair verification that the lamp is out and stays out.

How long it takes

Usually same-day. Sensor and wiring repairs are quick; a modulator or ECU depends on parts.

Worth knowing

Don't just reset it. An ABS lamp that comes back means the fault is still there, and a system that isn't working is one you find out about on wet pavement.

How the job runs

  • You call or send it in. Describe the symptom — the noise, the code, the light, what changed.
  • We diagnose and quote. Written estimate with labor, hours and parts broken out, before we start.
  • You approve, we work. If we find something else, you hear it before it lands on the invoice.
  • You leave with the paperwork. Itemized invoice and any documentation the job required.
