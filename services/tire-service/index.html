Truck Tire Service & Repair — East Dundee, IL

Tires are the cheapest thing to inspect and one of the most expensive to get wrong. We mount, repair and balance truck and trailer tires here in the shop, and we'll tell you why the last set wore the way it did.

Bring it in if you're seeing this

These are the words drivers use when they call us about tire service. If any of them describe your unit, describe it to us the same way on the phone — it gets us to the answer faster.

  • Uneven or rapid wear
  • Vibration at highway speed
  • Slow leak
  • Puncture or sidewall damage
  • Mismatched duals
  • Tread depth near legal limit
  • Casing separation or bulge
  • Wheel-end running hot

What we do

  • Mount, dismount and repair on tractor and trailer positions.
  • Balancing and vibration diagnosis.
  • Wheel and rim inspection, valve stems, torque to spec.
  • Wheel-end check while the wheel is off: seals, bearings, brake condition.
  • Wear-pattern read-out so the cause gets fixed, not just the tire.

How long it takes

Same-day. Tire work is scheduled around the rest of the job if you're in for something else.

Worth knowing

Bring it in for the wear pattern, not just the tire. Cupping, feathering and one-side wear each point at a different suspension or alignment cause, and reading them is free.

How the job runs

  • You call or send it in. Describe the symptom — the noise, the code, the light, what changed.
  • We diagnose and quote. Written estimate with labor, hours and parts broken out, before we start.
  • You approve, we work. If we find something else, you hear it before it lands on the invoice.
  • You leave with the paperwork. Itemized invoice and any documentation the job required.
