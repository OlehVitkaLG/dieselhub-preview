Bring it in if you're seeing this

These are the words drivers use when they call us about fuel systems. If any of them describe your unit, describe it to us the same way on the phone — it gets us to the answer faster.

  • Hard starting, especially cold
  • Rough idle or misfire
  • White or black smoke
  • Fuel mileage dropping
  • Loss of power under load
  • Fuel in the oil
  • Rail pressure codes
  • Visible fuel leak or smell

What we do

  • Rail pressure, return flow and cylinder contribution testing.
  • Injector testing, replacement and cup service.
  • High-pressure and transfer pump service.
  • Fuel lines, filter housings, water separators, heaters and tank issues.
  • Air-in-fuel and suction-side leak diagnosis — the cause of most "mystery" hard starts.

How long it takes

Filters and lines are same-day. Injector and pump work depends on the engine and on parts; you'll have the estimate and the timeline before we start.

Worth knowing

Cheap fuel and stretched filter intervals cause most of the injector work we see. If you're pushing filters well past the interval to save a few dollars, you're paying for it in injectors.

How the job runs

  • You call or send it in. Describe the symptom — the noise, the code, the light, what changed.
  • We diagnose and quote. Written estimate with labor, hours and parts broken out, before we start.
  • You approve, we work. If we find something else, you hear it before it lands on the invoice.
  • You leave with the paperwork. Itemized invoice and any documentation the job required.
