DPF & Aftertreatment Repair for Semi Trucks — East Dundee, IL

Aftertreatment is the number one reason modern trucks sit. A derate three states from home turns a load into a loss. We diagnose and repair the whole system — DPF, DOC, SCR, DEF and EGR — and because we're also a credentialed CARB Clean Truck Check tester, we can test it when the repair is done.

Bring it in if you're seeing this

These are the words drivers use when they call us about dpf & aftertreatment repair. If any of them describe your unit, describe it to us the same way on the phone — it gets us to the answer faster.

  • DPF light on or flashing
  • Regens failing or running constantly
  • Speed derate or 5 mph limp
  • DEF quality or level faults
  • SCR efficiency codes
  • NOx sensor codes
  • EGR cooler leaking or plugged
  • White smoke or raw fuel smell
  • Failed an emissions test

What we do

  • Full aftertreatment diagnosis: pressure, temperature and NOx sensor data, not just the code.
  • DPF and DOC service, cleaning and replacement.
  • SCR and DEF system repair: pumps, injectors, lines, heaters, quality sensors.
  • EGR valves, coolers and related plumbing.
  • Forced regens and post-repair verification.
  • CARB Clean Truck Check testing after the repair, in the same visit.

How long it takes

Sensor and DEF component work is often same-day. A plugged DPF, an EGR cooler or a full SCR job takes longer and depends on parts — the estimate says which.

Worth knowing

Deleting the aftertreatment is not a repair. It fails Clean Truck Check, it's illegal, and we won't do it. Fixing the system properly is cheaper than the fine and far cheaper than a truck that can't be registered.

How the job runs

  • You call or send it in. Describe the symptom — the noise, the code, the light, what changed.
  • We diagnose and quote. Written estimate with labor, hours and parts broken out, before we start.
  • You approve, we work. If we find something else, you hear it before it lands on the invoice.
  • You leave with the paperwork. Itemized invoice and any documentation the job required.
