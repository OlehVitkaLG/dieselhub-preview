What we don't do

Shops that say yes to everything waste your time. Here's the honest list, so you don't tow a unit here for nothing:

  • No roadside, no mobile, no towing. All work happens in our bays. If you're broken down on the shoulder, you need a tow first — we'll take it from there.
  • No collision or body work. Frame and panel damage goes to a body shop. Structural welding on trailers, though, we do.
  • No truck or trailer rental. We rent tow bars, which is a different thing.

How a job runs here

  • You call or send it in. Describe the symptom — the noise, the code, the light, what changed. Two minutes on the phone often narrows it down.
  • We diagnose and quote. You get a written estimate with labor, hours and parts broken out before we start.
  • You approve, we work. If we find something else in there, you hear about it before it lands on the invoice.
  • You leave with the paperwork. Itemized invoice, and the inspection or test documentation if the job needed it.
