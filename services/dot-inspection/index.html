DOT Inspections for Trucks & Trailers — East Dundee, IL

Your annual federal inspection is the one appointment you can plan around, and the one that costs the most when you don't. We run DOT inspections on tractors and trailers here in East Dundee, and if something fails, it gets fixed in the same building instead of sending you looking for another shop.

Bring it in if you're seeing this

These are the words drivers use when they call us about dot inspection. If any of them describe your unit, describe it to us the same way on the phone — it gets us to the answer faster.

  • Annual inspection due or expired
  • Sticker about to run out
  • Failed a roadside inspection
  • CSA score climbing
  • Just bought a used unit
  • Putting a trailer back in service

What we do

  • Full federal periodic inspection on the tractor, the trailer, or both.
  • Brakes, air system, steering, suspension, coupling, exhaust, lighting, tires and wheels, frame, glass and mirrors, emergency equipment.
  • A written list of anything out of spec, with what it costs to correct.
  • Corrections done here — brakes, air leaks, lighting, suspension, welding.
  • Documentation and sticker on completion.

How long it takes

A clean inspection is typically a same-day job. If something fails, the timeline depends on the correction and on parts — you'll get that estimate before we start the repair, not after.

Worth knowing

Bring both units if you run a tractor and trailer. Getting them inspected on the same visit saves you a second trip, and most drivers forget the trailer until the sticker is already expired.

How the job runs

  • You call or send it in. Describe the symptom — the noise, the code, the light, what changed.
  • We diagnose and quote. Written estimate with labor, hours and parts broken out, before we start.
  • You approve, we work. If we find something else, you hear it before it lands on the invoice.
  • You leave with the paperwork. Itemized invoice and any documentation the job required.
