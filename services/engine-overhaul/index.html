Diesel Engine Overhaul & Repair — East Dundee, IL

An overhaul is the biggest single invoice most owner-operators ever see on a truck, which is exactly why it should start with a straight conversation about whether the unit is worth it. We'll tell you what we find, what it costs, and what we'd do if it were our truck.

Bring it in if you're seeing this

These are the words drivers use when they call us about engine overhaul. If any of them describe your unit, describe it to us the same way on the phone — it gets us to the answer faster.

  • Blow-by out the breather
  • Oil consumption climbing
  • Low compression or hard starting
  • Coolant in the oil, oil in the coolant
  • Knocking or rod noise
  • Metal in the oil filter
  • Overheating under load
  • High mileage and it's time

What we do

  • Teardown and inspection with findings documented before the rebuild is quoted.
  • In-frame and out-of-frame overhauls: liners, pistons, rings, bearings, head work.
  • Head and valvetrain: valves, guides, seats, injector cups.
  • Oil cooler, water pump, turbo and related components addressed during the job.
  • Break-in and post-repair road test before you take it.

How long it takes

An overhaul is a multi-day job, and parts availability drives the schedule more than labor does. You get a timeline in writing with the estimate, and a call if it moves.

Worth knowing

Get the decision made before the engine makes it for you. A planned overhaul on your schedule costs far less than a failure on the road — the tow, the hotel and the lost load usually dwarf the difference.

How the job runs

  • You call or send it in. Describe the symptom — the noise, the code, the light, what changed.
  • We diagnose and quote. Written estimate with labor, hours and parts broken out, before we start.
  • You approve, we work. If we find something else, you hear it before it lands on the invoice.
  • You leave with the paperwork. Itemized invoice and any documentation the job required.
